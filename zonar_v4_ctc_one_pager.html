How it works
01 — Install
V4 connects to the OBD port
The V4 device installs directly on the vehicle and connects to the OBD system. OEM-installed hardware on compatible vehicles requires no additional installation.
02 — Collect
Emissions data captures continuously
During every route, the V4 monitors engine and emissions data in the background. Issues surface during normal operation — not on test day.
03 — Submit
Auto-submits to CARB on day one
At the start of each 90-day testing window, compliant data is submitted to CARB automatically. No action required from your team.
